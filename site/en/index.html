FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do the first contact and the quote look like?

You write or call and briefly describe what you need — we come back with clarifying questions and an initial quote. Scope and timeline are agreed individually, before any work starts.

Do you take on small jobs, or only large projects?

Both. A single short, one tool automating a specific process, or a larger rollout split into stages — we fit the scope to the need, not the other way round.

Do I need a ready specification to get started?

No. Some clients come with a finished idea, others just with a problem to solve. We define the scope and the details together at the start.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope of the project. We prepare an individual quote after talking through your needs — no rigid price lists stretched to fit the client.

How long does delivery take?

A single video is usually a matter of days; a larger automation rollout or a system takes a few weeks. You get a specific date once we have discussed the details of your project.

Do you work remotely, and do you stay after launch?

Yes — we work remotely, so your location does not matter. Developing and maintaining what we have delivered is a standard part of working with us, not an extra service.
